Six ways to pare back tab clutter.

Each action is explicit, reversible when Chrome allows it, and narrow enough to understand before you click.

01

Smart organize

Group eligible tabs in the current window by likely task or topic. Pinned, internal, and already grouped tabs stay untouched.

02

Duplicate cleanup

Keep the active, protected, or most recently used copy. Optionally ignore recognized tracking parameters when comparing URLs.

03

Close or Sleep

Close inactive tabs or ask Chrome to unload their pages while leaving each tab in place for your return.

04

Session-only Undo

Undo the latest organization or tab-closing cleanup during the current browser session. Retry only the tabs that could not be restored.

05

Protection and scope

Protect exact hostnames and choose whether manual cleanup covers every window or only the one where TabPare opened.

06

Optional automation

Automation is off by default. When enabled, it checks all windows and closes duplicate tabs only—never inactive tabs.

What TabPare leaves alone

Active. Pinned. Audible. Protected.

TabPare excludes Chrome internal pages and other non-web URLs. Smart organize processes only eligible tabs' titles and hostnames, using local matching and Chrome's on-device model only when it is already available.
