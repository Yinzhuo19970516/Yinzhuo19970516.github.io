Chrome tab cleanup

Keep the tabs that matter.

TabPare finds duplicate and inactive tabs, shows you the count, and closes them when you choose. Scheduled cleanup is available for duplicates only.

Private by designTab URLs are processed on your device and are not saved or sent to TabPare servers.

01 / SEE

Count first.

See how many duplicate and inactive tabs are candidates before starting either cleanup.

02 / CLOSE

One action.

Manual cleanup starts immediately. Active, pinned, and audible tabs remain protected.

03 / SCHEDULE

Your timing.

Automation is off by default. Two hours is preselected, with presets and custom intervals from 30 minutes to 7 days.

Predictable by default.

TabPare has one focused purpose: reduce open-tab clutter without inserting scripts into websites or sending browsing activity to a developer server.

  • Duplicate cleanupGroups matching HTTP or HTTPS URLs, keeps protected tabs, and otherwise keeps the most recently used copy.
  • Inactive cleanupUses the threshold you set for manual cleanup. Internal browser pages and active, pinned, or audible tabs are excluded.
  • Automatic cleanupRuns only after you enable it and closes duplicate tabs only. It never closes inactive tabs in the background.
